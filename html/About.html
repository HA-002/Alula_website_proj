About AlUla
Where History Meets Nature

Discover AlUla

AlUla, located in northwestern Saudi Arabia, is a captivating destination that seamlessly blends ancient heritage with breathtaking natural landscapes. Spanning over 22,000 square kilometers, AlUla is home to remarkable archaeological sites, including the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Hegra, the ancient city of Dadan, and the rock inscriptions of Jabal Ikmah. Visitors can also marvel at natural wonders like Elephant Rock and the stunning sandstone canyons.

Rich Heritage

Explore ancient tombs, inscriptions, and archaeological wonders that tell the story of civilizations past.

Stunning Nature

Experience the majestic landscapes, from towering rock formations to lush oases.

Adventure Awaits

Embark on thrilling activities like hiking, hot air ballooning, and stargazing under clear desert skies.
